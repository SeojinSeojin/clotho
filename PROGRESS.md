## Decisions (still in force)

**G4 counting-rule correction (2026-11-11, before new Sem-Ent results).** The existing E5 Decisions explicitly specifies ordering by absolute Spearman, but `order` and pairwise flips used signed coefficients. Restore the written rule: leaky ranking uses |rho|, leak-free ranking uses the seed mean of |rho|, pairwise signs use differences in those strengths (AUC unchanged).
