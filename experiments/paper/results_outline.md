# Results outline

ROC-AUC, fair_leakfree (label from samples 5-9, post-generation scores from samples 0-4), mean over 5 seeds of the macro mean; pairs are @100 / @200; "every seed" = paired difference of one sign on all 5. Llama-3.1-8B: 13 systems; Gemma-2-9B: 12.

**1. Failures are template-level.** With 40 extra samples per input, more than 92% of the reliable pass-rate variance lies between Loghub templates on 21 of 25 model x system pairs. Within a template every predictor is near chance against the 5-sample label. Against a clean 40-sample label Clotho rises from 0.555 to 0.609 (Llama; ceiling 0.698) and from 0.558 to 0.594 (Gemma; 0.727), so a small within-template signal exists (Figure within_template).

**2. Template-capped suites: Clotho beats lookups.** Clotho reaches 0.749 / 0.794 (Llama) and 0.780 / 0.832 (Gemma), ahead of MDSA and Lookup (Drain) on every seed (Table main).

**3. Natural logs: Drain lookup ties Clotho on AUC, but Clotho finds more failures per check at small budgets.** On uniform raw-line samples, Lookup (Drain) ties Clotho for Llama (-0.006 / -0.016) and beats it for Gemma (-0.037 / -0.057, every seed). With B labels plus B checks, Clotho finds more failures than a Drain-first strategy on every seed (Figure budget). The gain comes from active sampling: Clotho's labelled inputs hold more failures. Its ranking adds nothing beyond a Drain lookup on these suites. At B = 200, Drain-lookup ranking beats Clotho (+43 Llama, +111 Gemma failures). On capped suites both sampling and ranking contribute.

**4. Literature baselines.** LLMLog's selection score, used as a failure ranking (not its purpose), reaches 0.620 to 0.720, below Clotho on every seed in all four model x suite cells (A12 0.62 to 0.95). AdaParser's corrector, applied to the label samples, fixes 5.9 to 15.6% of failing inputs and breaks 97 to 219 passing ones. Against the corrected label Clotho keeps its AUC. Of the failures in Clotho's top 10%, only 2.1 to 4.9% are fixed, against 6.0 to 15.3% of all failures, so Clotho flags the failures the corrector cannot fix (Table literature).

**5. Cross-system transfer fails; a probe plus 25 to 50 target labels recovers it.** Clotho with references pooled from the other systems gets 0.44 to 0.66 on held-out systems. A logistic probe trained on the other systems' labels reaches 0.67 to 0.71 with no target labels. On identical target inputs, the protocol-chosen source model refit with target labels beats within-system Clotho @100 on every seed from k = 25 (Llama capped) or k = 50 (Llama natural, Gemma natural). On Gemma capped it only ties at k = 50 (Figure transfer).

**6. The original protocol leaks.** It computes pass labels and post-generation scores from the same 10 generations. Separating them lowers output entropy from 0.940 to 0.792 (Llama) at @100, a net leakage of +0.125 / +0.132 beyond the label-noise shift. Leak-free output entropy still needs 4 samples to match Clotho @100, at 1.5x to 2.4x Clotho's batched cost (Table leakage, Figure cost_auc).
