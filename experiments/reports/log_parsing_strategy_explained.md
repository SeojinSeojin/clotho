# Why Clotho finds more failures under a budget while Drain lookup ties it on AUC

Source: `log_parsing_strategy.py variants` and `report` (decomposition tables in `log_parsing_strategy_details.md`, `paper_numbers.md` section 12). Each cell crosses which B inputs get labelled (random, or Clotho's active sampling) with how the rest is ranked (random, Drain lookup on those labels, Clotho fitted on those labels). Failures are summed over systems, mean over 5 seeds; (n/5) = seeds with the same sign.

**Both, in proportions that depend on the suite.** Choosing what to label always helps: Clotho's actively sampled labelled set holds more failures than a random one at every budget (for example 928 vs 848 for Llama, natural, B = 100). With Clotho's ranking held fixed, it adds 36 to 96 failures on 5/5 seeds in 11 of 12 model x suite x budget cells (Gemma natural B = 200: +1). Ranking helps only on the template-capped suites: there, given the same labels, Clotho's ranking beats Drain-lookup ranking by 41 to 111 failures (5/5 in all six cells). On the natural suites it adds nothing beyond the lookup. With Clotho's labels, Drain-lookup ranking is within 13 failures of Clotho at B = 50 and 100, and ahead at B = 200 (+44 Llama, +68 Gemma, 5/5). Drain-lookup ranking with plain random labels trails Clotho at B = 50 and 100 (by 18 to 48), but beats it at B = 200 (+43 Llama, +111 Gemma, 5/5). So on naturally distributed logs, Clotho's budget advantage comes from active sampling (what it chooses to label). That matches the AUC tie, which measures only the ranking part. At larger budgets a Drain lookup ranking finds more failures than Clotho.
